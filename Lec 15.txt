--Bitwise Operators:

Complement, OR, XOR, Left Shift and Right Shift: 


--Complement: (~)(tilde) (It reverses the number of the binary digit)
>>> ~12
-13
>>> ~23
-24


--Binary format Bitwise:  XOR, AND(We use this symbol for AND &) , OR (We use this symbol (pipe) for OR |)
AND:
>>> 12 & 13
12
 >>> 25 & 30
24

OR:
>>> 12 | 13
13

XOR:
>>> 12 ^ 13
1
>>> 25 ^ 30
7

--Left Shift: (We use << for left shift)
>>> 10 << 2
40


--Right Shift: ((We use >> for right shift))
>>> 10 >> 2
2
