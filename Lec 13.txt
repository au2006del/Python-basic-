Swap two variables: we can simply use the formula: 
1) a= a+b
2) b= a-b
3) a =a-b
--Easy way in Pyrhon is (a,b) = (b,a)
--ROT_TWO() -> Is  the function that swaps the numbers in Python

>>> a= 5
>>> b= 6
>>> temp = a
>>> a
5
>>> b
6
>>> a= b
>>> b = temp
>>> a
6
>>> b
5
>>> a= a+b
>>> b= a-b
>>> a =a-b
>>> a
5
>>> b
6
